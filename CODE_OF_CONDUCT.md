# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for your mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to **support@lakshyapocketlab.com**. Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and may remove content or restrict participation to keep the community safe.

This text is attributed to the Contributor Covenant, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
